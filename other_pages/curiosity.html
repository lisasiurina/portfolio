Project Overview

Curiosity is a VR scenario being developed as part of my ongoing PhD research into emotion elicitation in virtual environments. I was awarded a Google PhD Fellowship in support of my doctoral research. The project explores how design patterns can elicit curiosity in VR. As the research is ongoing, this case study presents selected development materials and in-engine screenshots without disclosing the complete experience, specific hypotheses, or findings.

02. Experience Structure

The experience is structured across three acts: Setup, Confrontation, and Resolution. In this experience, confrontation is expressed through the search for the lens.

Curiosity begins in Act I, develops through the rising action of Act II, and reaches its peak at the climax of Act III.

06. Planned Evaluation

The Curiosity prototype will be evaluated through a randomized between-subjects study comparing sensory, narrative, and combined VR conditions. A neutral VR environment will be used to establish a physiological baseline.

Self-Report Measures

Self-Assessment Manikin (SAM) and Discrete Emotions Questionnaire (DEQ).

Physiological Measures

Electrodermal activity (EDA), heart rate variability (HRV), and respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA), recorded using the VU-AMS system.
